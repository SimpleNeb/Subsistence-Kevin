## NEW IN 0.2.5
Loot Boxes, Scrap Metal and Gather All add ordinary surface scavenging.
All includes eligible boxes and pickups such as fiber, scrap and berries; it
does not chop trees. Gather Wood remains the separate axe-based job.
Kevin searches a wider area and continues around his new position. Stay nearby;
the work still has distance limits and needs reachable surface terrain.
Hunting bows and recurve bows support defensive ordinary/premium arrows.
Swimming and base entry/interactions remain deferred.

## COMMANDS
Stand close + Use opens inventory. Crouch close + Use opens the command menu.
1 Follow Me. follows and runs when he falls behind.
2 Stay Here. waits where you leave him.
3 Inventory & Equipment. cargo, toolbelt, clothes and armor.
4 Gather Wood. requires a usable axe in cargo or toolbelt.
5 Gather Fiber. no tool required.
6 Loot Boxes. eligible ordinary world boxes.
7 Scrap Metal. surface scrap.
8 Gather All. eligible boxes and supported surface pickups, no chopping.
Click, press a menu number, or use arrows and Enter. Escape/right-click closes
the menu. Taking damage also closes it. Orders do not survive every interruption:
give a gathering order again after loading, revival, recall or defensive combat.

## SCAVENGING AND CAPACITY
Eligible boxes are ordinary surface ammo, medical and building-supplies crates.
Supported pickups are native fiber plants/patches, scrap, blueberries, rosehips,
strawberries, carrots, onions, potatoes, cotton, wheat, medicinal plants and
log piles. They must belong to the game's native surface vegetation pool.
No player/base/death storage, locked/special boxes, cave loot or underwater
resources are included. Dropped items, loose arrows and planted crops are
excluded; collect those yourself.

Kevin uses the real box inventory and native resource yields. Difficulty,
season and compatible native loot changes are respected. He takes only items
that fit in his cargo; leftover box items remain in the box. An item does not
become a second copy just because Kevin picked it up. Full cargo, no reachable
targets, terrain, distance from you or combat can stop the job. Stay nearby.
After making cargo room, issue the gathering command again to revisit leftovers.

Gather All includes eligible boxes and pickups; it does not cut trees. Give
Gather Wood separately, with a usable axe. Chopping wears the axe and respects
the game's depletion ledger. Fiber/pickups consume actual native world resources.
A wider search does not make every target reachable. Swimming/base access are
not added by this release.

## CARGO, CLOTHING AND DEFENSE
Kevin has 35 cargo slots, seven toolbelt slots and six clothing slots. Drag items
into him. Shift-click INTO Kevin is disabled; drag instead. Drag or shift-click
items back to yourself when you have room. Clothing and armor go in matching slots.
Put weapons and axes directly on the toolbelt, or leave one belt slot free so
Kevin can equip them from cargo.

Give a supported gun in cargo or toolbelt and matching ammunition:
M9 / Red9: 9mm. M37: shotgun shells. Double barrel: shells or slugs.
Rifle / lever-action rifle: rifle rounds. Revolver: .44 rounds.
Choose a supported ammo type on the gun before handing it over. Kevin does not
switch selected ammo types. Explosive/incendiary or unsupported combinations
are not used for defense, even when ordinary rounds are also in his inventory.

Transferred guns retain loaded ammo, upgrades and condition. The native gun
flashlight attachment retains charge. Other containers/nested storage are not
supported in Kevin's inventory. He defends against immediate threats, reloads
from actual carried ammunition and stops shooting when it runs out.
For bow defense, give Kevin a hunting bow or recurve bow in cargo or toolbelt
and matching ordinary or premium arrows. Select the arrow type on the bow
before handing it over; he does not switch types. Drawing takes time, arrows
come from his actual inventory, and he stops when they run out. Grenade arrows
are not supported. Keep clear of his firing line.

## HUD RECALL
The upper-right HUD shows status and F6: RECALL KEVIN. Close other menus, press
F6 to free the cursor, then click when the label reads CLICK: RECALL KEVIN.
F6, Escape or right-click cancels the cursor.

Living Kevin returns beside you with the same health and belongings. Recall
cancels his work and resumes Follow. Dead recall brings him back at half health
with an empty inventory. His gear remains in the original death bag; recover it
separately before normal bag expiry. Recall does not retrieve or copy that gear.

You must be alive and have a clear, safe spot on outdoor surface ground nearby.
If unavailable, close other menus and move away from caves, water, buildings
or obstacles. The HUD explains when recall cannot be used.

## OTHER REVIVAL OPTIONS
Stand + Use on the death bag to loot it. Crouch + Use to revive Kevin there, or
wait about two minutes while the world runs for him to return near you on safe
outdoor ground. Both return him at half health with empty inventory. No gear is
copied; lack of a safe place delays return. Saving preserves remaining time.
The countdown pauses while Kevin is disabled or you are dead.

## SAVING AND DISABLING
Kevin's belongings, health, location and Follow/Stay order are saved with the
profile. Give gathering orders again after loading. Remove him from a profile's
Mods selection to disable him there. Globally, close the game and choose Disable
Kevin in Setup. Install / Update enables him again.

Keep the game's .KevinLoader folder and KevinCompanion.u for existing saves.
They hold original-file backups/install records and the classes those saves use.
Installer/Disable operations do not rewrite your save files.

## INSTALLATION HELP
Setup is unsigned. Smart App Control can block it and has no individual-app
exception. Keep protection enabled; do not add an exclusion. Report the exact
warning, security product, detection name and Setup version. A clean result from
one product does not settle another product's detection. Microsoft explains:
https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/security/threat-malware-protection/smart-app-control-frequently-asked-questions

If Setup opens but reports game-folder access denied, Retry as administrator
requests permission to change that folder. It does not resolve a security block.
For other errors, use Save setup report. Logs are also under
%LOCALAPPDATA%\KevinCompanion\Logs. Remove personal folder names before posting.
If Setup never opens, include the warning screenshot. Do not force a patch over
unsupported game versions or changed files. Reports and updates:
https://github.com/SimpleNeb/Subsistence-Kevin/issues
