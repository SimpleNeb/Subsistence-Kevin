SIMPLE FRAMEWORK

Connect once.
Then just play.

Start the one-time connection from Subsistence's main menu. These two buttons copy exactly the command you need.

Already connected? You can skip these commands. In Subsistence, open Options > Check Mods for updates and repair.
Before you start

Subscribe, download, open the game.

With Subsistence closed, subscribe to Simple Framework and your chosen mods. Let Steam finish downloading. Open Subsistence and stay at the main menu.

First command

Start the connection.

Press = at the main menu. Copy this command, paste with Ctrl+V, then press Enter.

exec ../../../workshop/content/418030/3824274376/SF-CONNECT.txt

Press Esc until the console closes, then choose Exit Game.

Second command

Reopen the game and finish connecting.

Stay at the main menu. Press =, paste the second command and press Enter.

exec ../../../workshop/content/418030/3824274376/SF-CONNECT-OPEN.txt

The connection helper opens and the game closes. Follow the helper. If it asks, choose Steam > Exit, then continue.

You're connected

Choose your mods and play.

When the helper confirms the connection, reopen Steam and Subsistence. Enable Simple Framework and your chosen mods, then load into your world.

Later, open Options > Check Mods whenever you need the Simple Framework Control Panel.
