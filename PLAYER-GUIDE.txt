KEVIN COMPANION — 0.2.2 PREVIEW GUIDE

A FRIEND IN THE WILDERNESS
Kevin joins you automatically when his mod is selected. No recruitment quest:
start or continue a supported single-player world and find him by your side.

INSTALL
Close Subsistence. Open Kevin Setup supplied with this release,
and choose Install / Update. Setup finds your Steam game folder; Browse lets you
select it if needed. Then start Subsistence, select Kevin Companion in your
profile's Mods list, and start or continue that profile.

A Workshop release still requires Setup to install Kevin's companion code.
Subscription alone is not enough. Project downloads belong at:
github.com/SimpleNeb/Subsistence-Kevin

TALK TO KEVIN
Stand close and press your normal Use key to open his inventory.
Crouch and press Use to open his command menu:
  Follow Me — Kevin follows and runs when he falls behind.
  Stay Here — Kevin waits where you leave him.
  Inventory & Equipment — open his cargo, weapons and clothing.
  Gather Wood — Kevin needs a usable axe in his inventory or toolbelt.
  Gather Fiber — collect nearby fiber plants; no tool required.
Click a command, press 1–5, or use the arrow keys and Enter.
Escape or right-click closes the menu. Taking damage also closes it.

CARRYING AND EQUIPMENT
Kevin has 35 cargo slots, seven toolbelt slots and six clothing slots.
Drag items into his inventory; shift-click into Kevin is disabled. To take items
back, drag them out or shift-click them into your inventory if you have room.
Put clothing and armor in the matching clothing slots. Items containing their
own storage cannot be placed in Kevin's inventory.

Put a supported firearm in his toolbelt and supply its matching ammunition:
  M9 or Red9 — 9mm rounds.
  M37 shotgun — shotgun shells.
  Double-barrel shotgun — shotgun shells or shotgun slugs.
  Rifle or lever-action rifle — rifle rounds.
  Revolver — .44 rounds.
Kevin reloads from the ammunition he actually carries. Explosive, incendiary,
and other unsupported weapon/ammunition combinations are not used for defense.
His ammunition and axe condition are finite. He defends himself and you against
immediate threats. Gathering fills cargo slots and stops when full.

WOOD AND FIBER
Kevin gathers nearby resources while you remain close. In 0.2.2, he searches
2.5 times farther than before and searches around his current position whenever
he chooses the next resource. This helps him continue into nearby patches. He
still works within a limited distance of you.

Wood comes from actual trees and follows the player's tree-depletion record.
His axe wears with use. Fiber comes from actual plants, which are consumed.
A full cargo inventory stops gathering before a resource is consumed. Give a
new order to change his job.

A wider search does not make every resource reachable. Terrain, blocked routes,
a broken axe, full cargo or moving too far away can stop the job. When no
reachable resources remain in range, Kevin stops gathering and follows you.
Move near another patch and give the gathering order again when needed.
Gathering jobs do not resume after a reload, revival, or combat interruption.
Open the menu and give the order again when you are ready.

IF KEVIN DIES
His belongings stay in his dropped loot bag.
Crouch and press Use on that bag to revive him nearby, or wait about two minutes
while the world is running for him to return near you on safe outdoor ground. He returns with
half health and an empty inventory. Retrieve his belongings from the old bag;
revival never duplicates them. Stand and use the bag for normal looting.
Saving and quitting preserves his remaining return time. The countdown pauses
while Kevin is disabled or the player is dead. The normal game's bag expiry
still applies, so collect valuable equipment promptly.

DISABLE OR UPDATE
Close the game and open Setup again. Disable Kevin restores the original game
startup files and retains Kevin's compatibility package and saved belongings.
Install / Update enables the supported build again. Keep the .KevinLoader folder
in the game directory: it contains the original-file backups and install record.
Use the new installer for this update. It checks whether your earlier Kevin
installation is supported before changing files.

PREVIEW LIMITS
Windows, standalone single-player, Subsistence Alpha 68.19 only.
Multiplayer is not supported. Movement and gathering are intended for outdoor
surface terrain; following pauses around bases and caves. There is no teleport
catch-up. Obstacles and terrain may require you to reposition or repeat an order.
Unsupported game versions and conflicting file changes are refused by Setup.
After a game update, use a matching Kevin release rather than forcing a patch.
This is an unofficial community mod, not affiliated with Subsistence's developer.

Setup is an unsigned preview. Keep its accompanying SHA256 checksum with the
download. A ZIP containing the same installer is available as a fallback.
